Big 4 Financial Risk Analysis

Overview:
This project analyzes financial risk and compliance data for the Big 4 accounting firms – PwC, Deloitte, KPMG, and Ernst & Young – covering the period from 2020 to 2025. The analysis examines key metrics such as high risk cases, compliance violations, fraud cases detected, and total revenue impact to understand trends and differences among these firms.

Data:
• Dataset: Big 4 Financial Risk Insights (2020–2025) from Kaggle.
• Metrics include: Year; Firm Name; Total Audit Engagements; High Risk Cases; Compliance Violations; Fraud Cases Detected; Industry Affected; Total Revenue Impact; AI Used for Auditing; Employee Workload; Audit Effectiveness Score; and Client Satisfaction Score.

Methodology:
• Data was imported and inspected using Python (Pandas) to ensure accuracy and consistency.
• Basic cleaning and formatting were applied (e.g. checking for missing values and confirming data types).
• Trend and correlation analyses were performed using Seaborn and Matplotlib to visualize changes over time and compare metrics across firms.
• Key insights were summarised and future recommendations were developed based on the findings.

Key Findings:
• High Risk Cases ranged from approximately 200 to 450, with Deloitte experiencing a significant spike around 2023.
• Compliance Violations were highest for Ernst & Young (around 300 on average), while PwC maintained lower numbers (approximately 200).
• Fraud Cases showed a moderate correlation (about 0.27) with total audit engagements, suggesting that more audits may uncover additional fraud cases.
• Total Revenue Impact demonstrated a mild upward trend from 2020 to 2025, indicating gradual improvements likely due to market conditions or effective client retention strategies.

• Embrace Advanced Analytics:

Invest in predictive modelling and machine learning tools to analyse historical data and forecast future risks.
